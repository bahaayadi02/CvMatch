Machine Learning Engineer
Berlin · Full-time

About the role
We are hiring a Machine Learning Engineer to join our recommendation systems team.
You will design, train, and deploy production ML models that serve millions of users daily.

What you'll do
- Build end-to-end machine learning pipelines in Python using scikit-learn and PyTorch.
- Train deep learning models for ranking and recommendation; iterate based on offline metrics and online A/B tests.
- Deploy models behind low-latency services using Docker, Kubernetes, and AWS.
- Set up CI/CD pipelines and monitor model performance in production.
- Collaborate with data scientists, software engineers, and product managers.

Requirements
- 2+ years of professional experience as an ML or data engineer.
- Strong Python skills; comfortable with pandas, NumPy, and at least one deep learning framework (PyTorch or TensorFlow).
- Working knowledge of SQL and at least one cloud platform (AWS, GCP, or Azure).
- Familiar with Docker, Git, and Linux.
- Solid foundation in machine learning, statistics, and feature engineering.

Nice to have
- Experience deploying models with FastAPI or similar.
- Exposure to MLOps tooling (MLflow, Weights & Biases).
- Background in NLP or computer vision.
