Embedded Systems Engineer
Stuttgart · Full-time

The role
We design and build hardware for industrial automation. We're looking for an embedded
engineer to develop firmware and signal-processing pipelines on our next-generation controller.

What you'll do
- Develop firmware in C and C++ for ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers.
- Implement real-time signal-processing algorithms; some work on FPGA in VHDL or Verilog.
- Design and review schematics and PCB layouts.
- Work with communication protocols including I2C, SPI, UART, CAN, and Modbus.
- Debug hardware and software using oscilloscopes, logic analysers, and JTAG.

Required
- B.Sc/M.Sc in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, or related.
- 2+ years of professional embedded development experience.
- Strong C/C++; familiarity with microcontrollers and real-time operating systems.
- Comfort with Linux, Git, and shell scripting.
- Knowledge of analog and digital electronics.

Nice to have
- Experience with FPGA design (VHDL or Verilog).
- IoT experience (MQTT, ESP32, etc.).
- MATLAB for algorithm prototyping.
