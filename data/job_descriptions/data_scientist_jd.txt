Data Scientist
Munich · Hybrid

The role
We're looking for a data scientist to work on customer analytics and pricing models for our
e-commerce platform.

Responsibilities
- Conduct exploratory data analysis on large transaction datasets using Python (pandas, NumPy).
- Build statistical and machine learning models for customer segmentation and demand forecasting.
- Design and analyse A/B tests; present findings to non-technical stakeholders.
- Write efficient SQL queries against our data warehouse.
- Build interactive dashboards and visualisations for the business team.

Required skills
- 2+ years in a data science or analytics role.
- Strong proficiency in Python and SQL.
- Solid grounding in statistics, hypothesis testing, and machine learning.
- Experience with scikit-learn, pandas, and a visualisation library (matplotlib, seaborn, or Plotly).
- Excellent communication; able to explain results to non-technical audiences.

Nice to have
- Experience with R, XGBoost, or LightGBM.
- Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS or GCP) and Git.
- Experience with dashboarding tools such as Streamlit, Tableau, or Looker.
