DevOps Engineer
Frankfurt · Full-time

About the role
We are looking for a DevOps engineer to build and maintain our cloud infrastructure
and deployment pipelines across AWS and GCP.

Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain CI/CD pipelines for microservice deployments.
- Manage Kubernetes clusters, Helm charts, and container orchestration.
- Automate infrastructure provisioning using Terraform and Ansible.
- Set up monitoring, logging, and alerting with Prometheus and Grafana.
- Containerise applications with Docker; troubleshoot production incidents.
- Collaborate with development teams to improve deployment velocity and reliability.

Required skills
- 3+ years of professional DevOps or SRE experience.
- Strong knowledge of Docker, Kubernetes, and at least one cloud platform (AWS or GCP).
- Experience with Terraform or similar infrastructure-as-code tools.
- Solid Linux administration and shell scripting (Bash).
- Familiarity with CI/CD tools (Jenkins, GitHub Actions, or GitLab CI).
- Good understanding of networking fundamentals.

Nice to have
- Experience with Go or Python for tooling and automation.
- Ansible or configuration management experience.
- Exposure to service mesh (Istio, Linkerd).
